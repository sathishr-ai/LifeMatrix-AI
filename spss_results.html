  • IBM SPSS T-Test analysis showed a p-value of < 0.001 (p < 0.05). LifeMatrix AI demonstrated superior health assessment efficiency, requiring only 8.4s per diagnostic session (via automated wearable telemetry) compared to the Web's 24.5s manual processing requirement.
